Welcome to the blog! This is the first post — a quick introduction to what you can expect here.

## Why a blog?

I've spent over a decade designing products, and along the way I've collected a lot of thoughts about what makes digital experiences work (and what doesn't). This is a place to share some of those ideas.

## What I'll write about

- **Product design strategy** — balancing user needs with business goals
- **AI in UX** — practical lessons from shipping AI-powered features
- **Design leadership** — building practice, mentoring, and cross-team collaboration
- **Accessibility** — because great products work for everyone
- **The craft** — typography, interaction patterns, and the details that matter

## Stay tuned

No set schedule — just good posts when there's something worth saying. If you want to chat about anything here, [drop me an email](mailto:oli_smart@hotmail.com).
